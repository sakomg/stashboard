    Put your whole stash on one board

    Add the wallets and exchanges you hold crypto in. Stashboard reads their balances, merges them into one portfolio and keeps its history. It only reads: a wallet needs its public address, an exchange a read-only API key.

    1. Balances are read as soon as an account is added, usually within a minute.
    2. History begins with the next scheduled update.
    3. Changes over 24 hours, 7 and 30 days fill in as the history grows.
